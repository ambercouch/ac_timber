About Cale & Co.

Reliable help with your home and garden.

Quality work without the hassle.

Friendly, practical help for the decorating, maintenance and improvement jobs that keep your home looking and working as it should.

Cale & Co. provides home improvement, property maintenance and general repair services for homeowners across Bridgend, Pontyclun and the surrounding areas.

Whether you need a room redecorated, a fence replaced, a small patio laid or simply have a list of jobs that need taking care of, Sion can help.

The emphasis is on a friendly, straightforward service with clear pricing and work completed carefully, tidily and with as little hassle as possible. Smaller jobs can be undertaken on an hourly basis, while larger projects can be assessed and quoted individually.

Need something done that is not listed? Get in touch. All types of home improvement and maintenance work can be considered.
